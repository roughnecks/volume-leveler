# Privacy Policy for Volume Leveler

**Last updated:** 12/09/2026

Volume Leveler ("the app") is developed by Simone Canaletti ("the developer", "I", "we"). This policy explains what information the app accesses and, more importantly, what it does not do with it.

## Summary

Volume Leveler does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device and never connects to the internet.

## What the app accesses, and why

To automatically balance the volume of your audio applications, Volume Leveler needs to see which applications on your PC are currently producing audio, so it can identify and adjust them. To do this, the app
reads:

- The list of running processes that are currently playing audio (application names only).
- The audio volume and audio stream data of those processes, via standard Windows audio APIs (WASAPI), solely in order to measure loudness and apply volume corrections.

This information is used exclusively, in real time, on your own device, to perform the app's core function. It is never written to disk beyond your local settings file (see below), never transmitted anywhere,
and never seen by the developer.

## Local settings storage

The app saves your preferences (such as your chosen reference volume level, blacklisted applications, and window position) in a local settings file on your own device. This file never leaves your device and is
 not accessible to the developer.

## No telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting

Volume Leveler does not include any telemetry, usage analytics, or automated crash reporting. The app does not check for updates automatically and does not make any network connections.

## No accounts, no third-party data sharing

The app does not require or support user accounts, sign-in, or any form of authentication. Since no data is collected, none is ever shared with, or sold to, third parties.

## Donations

The app's companion website (volumeleveler.app) may link to third-party donation platforms (such as Ko-fi, LiberaPay, or PayPal.me) for people who wish to voluntarily support development. These are independent
 services with their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave volumeleveler.app or interact with them directly. The developer does not receive any personal information from these platforms beyond what
they choose to show for the purposes of processing a voluntary donation.

## Children's privacy

Volume Leveler is a general-purpose Windows utility and is not directed at children. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, this applies equally to users of any age.
